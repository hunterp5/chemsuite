chemsuite permeability models
=============================

The model files in this folder (`*.csqm`) are part of chemsuite and are licensed
GPL-3.0-or-later, the same as the rest of chemsuite. See the COPYING file at the root of
the distribution for the full text.

Each file is a fitted model: an uncompressed NumPy `.npz` archive holding a JSON manifest
and plain numeric arrays, read with `allow_pickle=False`. Opening one never executes code.
A `.csqm` also carries the identifiers (InChIKeys) and measured values of the compounds it
was fitted on, so that the model can honestly be refined later with more data. Those
compounds and values come from the public sources below.

How they were made is recorded in docs/PERMEABILITY_PLAN.md, and the scripts that made
them are scripts/permeability/ — `fetch.py` (which pins every source file by SHA-256),
`curate.py` and `train.py`.


Training data
-------------

1. mdck_er.csqm — MDR1-MDCK efflux ratio

   Fang, C.; Wang, Y.; Grater, R.; Kapadnis, S.; Black, C.; Trapa, P.; Sciabola, S.
   "Prospective Validation of Machine Learning Algorithms for Absorption, Distribution,
   Metabolism, and Excretion Prediction: An Industrial Perspective."
   J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2023, 63 (11), 3263-3274. doi:10.1021/acs.jcim.3c00160

   Data: https://github.com/molecularinformatics/Computational-ADME
   The `LOG MDR1-MDCK ER (B-A/A-B)` column of `ADME_public_set_3521.csv`.

   That dataset is distributed under the MIT License, a copy of which is in this folder as
   LICENSE-biogen-adme.txt. Its copyright notice is retained there as the licence requires.

2. caco2.csqm — Caco-2 apparent permeability

   Wang, N.-N.; Dong, J.; Deng, Y.-H.; Zhu, M.-F.; Wen, M.; Yao, Z.-J.; Lu, A.-P.;
   Wang, J.-B.; Cao, D.-S. "ADME Properties Evaluation in Drug Discovery: Prediction of
   Caco-2 Cell Permeability Using a Combination of NSGA-II and Boosting."
   J. Chem. Inf. Model. 2016, 56 (4), 763-773. doi:10.1021/acs.jcim.5b00642

   Obtained through Therapeutics Data Commons as the `caco2_wang` dataset:
   Huang, K.; Fu, T.; Gao, W.; et al. "Therapeutics Data Commons: Machine Learning
   Datasets and Tasks for Drug Discovery and Development." NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and
   Benchmarks. https://tdcommons.ai

   The measurements are experimental values reported in the paper's supporting
   information and redistributed by TDC. They are facts rather than creative expression,
   and the citations above are given so that the provenance travels with the model.


Not used
--------

A PAMPA model was fitted on the NCATS PAMPA pH 5 data (via Therapeutics Data Commons,
`pampa_ncats`; work of US government employees and so not subject to copyright) and is
deliberately not shipped: it could rank a series but not reliably classify an individual
compound. docs/PERMEABILITY_PLAN.md records the measured scores.

No data from iBonD, DataWarrior, GOSTAR or any other restricted compilation was used, and
no proprietary or licensed dataset contributed to any model in this folder.
