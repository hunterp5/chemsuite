chemsuite pKa weights, release 1 (chemsuite-pka-1.npz)
Copyright (C) 2026 Hunter Picard

The weights of chemsuite's own pKa model (chemsuite pKa), shipped with chemsuite for Predict
pKa, Standardize, the pKa descriptors and the ligand states in Prepare. They are one fold of a
5-fold training, at 20 epochs.

License: GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version, the
same as chemsuite. See chemsuite's LICENSE.

The weights were fine-tuned from Uni-Mol's published pretrained backbone
(mol_pre_all_h_220816.pt; Zhou et al., ICLR 2023), Copyright (c) 2022 DP Technology, under the
MIT License. See LICENSE-Uni-Mol.txt.

- Method: Uni-pKa (Luo et al., JACS Au 2024; https://github.com/dptech-corp/Uni-pKa,
  Apache-2.0). These are not the published Uni-pKa weights.
- Training data: the aqueous pKa values of pKaHub (Sipos-Szabo et al., JCIM 2026), from its
  redistributable sources only. No iBonD, DataWarrior or IUPAC data were used.
