chemsuite's sites-of-metabolism feature set (chemsuite/metabolism/features.py) is ported from
som-benchmark (https://github.com/WillDeSnoo/som-benchmark), an independent comparison of
atom-level CYP site-of-metabolism models, whose licence follows. The code reused is its
`basic_atom_block` and `charge_block` featurization and the hyperparameter grid and protocol of
its `_fit_gbm`; chemsuite's weights are its own, trained by scripts/som/train_gbm.py, and are
released under chemsuite's own licence (GPL-3.0-or-later).

The site-of-metabolism measurements used to train them are not distributed here or by
som-benchmark; they come from the Zaretzki et al. dataset as redistributed by ATTNSOM. Nothing
derived from them other than the fitted trees is shipped.
